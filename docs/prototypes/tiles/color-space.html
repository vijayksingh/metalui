Tile space: colour, feeling and sound from one position

A third axis, Weight, joins the plane. It measures how much the act matters: whether it can be undone, whether it commits you. A tile's position in this space now sets three things together: its colours, the feeling it names, and the sound it makes when it acts. Turn on sound, then hover a tile, click any point in the space, or move the sliders.
