Each is one recipe for the look (lightness range, gloss, grain, flecks, translucency, bevel) and one for the sound (the modes it rings at when struck, its attack, its contact noise). The beeper: the only source of tones

A beeper is a real Part: a small grille over a piezo, on tiles that report a state. Only state changes play it, never acts or hover. Its timbre is a piezo's: a sine with a 3.2 kHz resonance and a little square. Major and rising means good news; low, minor and rough means failure.
