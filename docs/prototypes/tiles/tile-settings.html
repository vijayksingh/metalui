One tile, lit properly

Settings as a fader bank. One light from the upper left. The panel is a thick slab with rolled edges; the slots and the LED are cut into it. The caps are blocks standing on it, and each casts a soft shadow down and to the right. Grain is a slight roughness in the surface that the light picks up, plus sparse flecks. Shown at 320, 160, 64 and 32. Hover or tap: the faders move to a new mix, and the LED flickers with the activity, then settles bright.
